Documents for form-filling and counselling

Bring for form-filling

  • Class 10 marksheet or certificate. Check spelling and date of birth against your ID.
  • Class 12 board, school, subject and roll or registration details if issued. Bring the marksheet if you have already passed.
  • Recent photo, scanned signature, and Aadhaar or another accepted ID. Bring a phone for OTPs and live photo capture.
  • Category, EWS, OBC-NCL, PwD or UDID documents where applicable. Use the current bulletin for accepted formats and validity dates.

Keep ready for counselling

  • Scorecard, Class 10 and 12 records, school leaving certificate, and domicile or nationality evidence when required.
  • Maharashtra candidates should check DigiLocker, APAAR and state quota requirements. Reserved category claims may require caste validity or other state certificates.
  • Marine applicants should check eyesight, colour vision, medical fitness, passport and DNS sponsorship requirements for their programme.
  • Download the confirmation page and fee receipt for every completed application. Enter OTPs and payment details yourself.
